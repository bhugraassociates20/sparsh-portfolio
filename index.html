Healthcare AI · Published research

Diabetic Foot Ulcer Detection

Problem

Diabetic foot ulcers escalate to amputation when caught late, and screening depends on specialist availability. Single-stage classifiers say whether an ulcer exists but not where.

What I built

A two-stage architecture: a CNN first categorises the wound image, then a region proposal network localises the ulcer boundary — giving clinicians a decision and the evidence for it.

Outcome

92.8% classification and 90.5% detection accuracy. Published by Taylor & Francis in Recent Advances in Computing Sciences.

  • CNN
  • Region Proposal Network
  • Medical imaging
  • PyTorch

Healthcare AI · Computer vision

Brain Tumour Detection Suite

Problem

Radiologists need both localisation and classification from an MRI slice. A detector alone misses tumour type; a classifier alone gives no region to review.

What I built

A dual-branch system that runs YOLOv7 for localisation in parallel with Inception and EfficientNet classifiers, then reconciles them into a single ranked read.

Why the ensemble

The branches fail differently — detection struggles with diffuse margins, classification with small lesions. Combining them raised agreement on the ambiguous cases that matter clinically.

  • YOLOv7
  • Inception
  • EfficientNet
  • Ensembling
